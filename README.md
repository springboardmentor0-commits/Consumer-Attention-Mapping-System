# Consumer Attention Mapping System
# Overview

The Consumer Attention Mapping System is an AI-powered retail analytics platform that uses Computer Vision, Artificial Intelligence, and Machine Learning to analyze customer behavior inside retail stores. The system processes live CCTV video streams to detect and track shoppers, analyze their attention towards products and shelves, monitor product interactions, and generate actionable business insights.

By understanding customer movement, shopping patterns, and product engagement, the platform helps retailers optimize store layouts, improve product placement, enhance customer experience, and increase sales through data-driven decision-making.

# Key Features
Real-time customer detection and tracking
Customer movement and journey analysis
Product attention and dwell-time analysis
Product pickup and interaction detection
Consumer behavior analysis and segmentation
Store and shelf attention heatmaps
Product attractiveness scoring
AI-based shelf optimization recommendations
Interactive dashboards and analytics
PDF and Excel report generation

# Tech Stack
